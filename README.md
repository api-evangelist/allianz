## APIs

Allianz does not publish a central public developer portal or developer API. Distribution and partner integrations are run at the operating-entity level via bilateral agreements; embedded-insurance and assistance partners integrate through Allianz Partners' partner programs rather than a self-service public API.

## Features

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| P&C Insurance | Property & casualty insurance (motor, home, business) underwritten by Allianz operating entities across 70+ countries. |
| Life & Health Insurance | Life, health, and pensions underwriting under Allianz Lebensversicherung and equivalent regional entities. |
| Asset Management | Asset management through Allianz Global Investors (AllianzGI) and PIMCO. |
| Travel Insurance and Assistance | Travel insurance and global emergency assistance delivered by Allianz Partners / Allianz Travel. |
| Specialty Lines | Allianz Commercial / AGCS provides specialty corporate and large-account coverage. |

## Compliance

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Solvency II | EU Solvency II directive governs capital adequacy, risk management, and reporting. |
| IFRS 17 | IFRS 17 insurance-contract accounting applies to Allianz Group reporting. |
| GDPR | General Data Protection Regulation compliance for personal data across European operations. |
| NAIC (US) | U.S. operating subsidiaries are regulated by individual state insurance commissioners under the NAIC framework. |
| BaFin Supervision | BaFin (German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority) supervises Allianz SE at the group level. |
